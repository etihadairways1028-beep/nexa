Privacy Policy

Nexa — www.nexaconnect.qd.je · Last updated 13 October 2026
In short: Nexa uses your information only to run the app for you — to sign you in, deliver your messages and calls, and send your notifications. We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and you can delete your account at any time.

Who can use Nexa

Nexa is for people aged 13 and over. If you are under the age required in your country to agree to this on your own, please use Nexa only with a parent's or guardian's permission.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, display name, @username, and (if you add them) a profile photo, banner, bio, birthday (month and day only), profile style and favourite games.
  • What you share: the messages, photos, videos, voice messages, polls, events, moments and other content you send or post, and who you send them to.
  • Status: whether you're online and when you were last active (you can hide this in Settings › Privacy), and your "Playing / Listening / Watching" activity if you choose to set one.
  • Notifications: a notification token for each device where you allow notifications, so we can send your messages and calls to it.
  • Calls: voice and video calls connect directly between devices, or through a relay server when a direct connection isn't possible. Calls are not recorded.
  • Nexa for computer (optional): if you turn on "Detect my games", the computer app checks the names of running programs on your computer against a list of games, only to show which game you're playing. The list of programs never leaves your computer — only the name of a matching game is shared, and only with your friends.
  • Reports: if you report someone, we keep the report and the reported message so the Nexa Support team can review it.

How we use it

  • To run Nexa: sign-in, delivering messages and calls, showing your profile to the people you choose, and sending notifications.
  • To keep people safe: reviewing reports, blocking abuse and enforcing our rules. Photos may be checked automatically for unsafe content when they are sent, so they can be blurred.
  • To answer you when you contact Nexa Support, and to send important emails about your account (for example a sign-in code or a new version of Nexa).

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with advertisers.

Where it's stored

Nexa runs on Google Firebase (accounts, database and notifications) and Cloudflare (website and server). Your information is stored on their servers and protected by their security. Call relay is provided by Metered. These providers process data only to run Nexa for us.

Who can see your information

  • Your name, @username, photo and bio can be seen by other signed-in Nexa users. Your messages can be seen by the people in that chat.
  • Friends see your online status and activity (unless you turn them off).
  • The Nexa Support team can see help chats you start with them, and reports.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. Disappearing messages and moments are removed when they expire. When you delete your account, your profile, username, friendships and the events you host are deleted.

Your choices

  • Change your profile, privacy and notification settings any time in Settings.
  • Delete your account in Settings › Account › Delete account.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or ask us to delete it, by emailing us.

Contact

Questions about privacy: nexaconnectofficial@gmail.com, or message @support in the app.

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and let you know in the app.
