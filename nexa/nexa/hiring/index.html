Our standards

The Nexa Team sets the example.

Everyone on the team agrees to the Terms & Conditions and to these standards.

💙
No bullying. No swearing. No offensive language.
Rule number 1 on Nexa, and on the Nexa Team.
  • Keep private messages, reports and personal information confidential, always.
  • Be fair and respectful to everyone, whoever they are.
  • Never ask anyone for passwords, codes or personal details.
  • Never share or copy Nexa’s design, code or unreleased features.
  • Under 18? You’ll need a parent or guardian’s permission to volunteer.
Questions

Good to know.

Do I need a Nexa account?

Yes. You apply inside Nexa, so we know who we’re talking to and can reply in your chat. Making an account is free and takes about a minute.

Is this a paid job?

No. All Nexa Team roles are volunteer roles, so they are unpaid. You get a Team badge, early access to new features and real experience working on an app.

Do I need experience?

No. We care more about kindness, reliability and wanting to help. For the Designer & Video Creator role, it helps to share a few things you’ve made.

How much time do I need?

As little as 1–2 hours a week. You choose when; there are no fixed shifts.

How old do I need to be?

You need to be 13 or older to use Nexa and to apply. If you’re under 18, a parent or guardian needs to be OK with you volunteering.

Can I apply for more than one role?

Yes. Send one application per role, or mention the other roles in your answer.

What happens to my information?

Only the Nexa Team sees your application, and we use it only to review it and contact you. See the Privacy Policy.
